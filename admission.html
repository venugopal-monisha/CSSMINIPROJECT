Step-by-Step Admission Process

  1. Visit the school office or call us for basic details.
  2. Collect the admission form from the office or download from the school.
  3. Fill the form carefully with correct details of the student and parents.
  4. Attach all required documents (see list below).
  5. Submit the form in the office within the given dates.
  6. Attend interaction / entrance test if required.
  7. Pay the admission fee after confirmation.

Important Dates (Tentative)

Activity Approx. Date

Documents Required

  • Birth certificate of the student
  • Previous school transfer certificate (if applicable)
  • Previous class report card
  • Aadhar card photocopy
  • Two recent passport size photographs

Eligibility

LKG: Minimum age 3 years as on 1st June
UKG: Minimum age 4 years as on 1st June
Class 1 and above: Based on previous class passed and vacancy.

Simple FAQs

Q: Is there any entrance exam?

A: For lower classes (LKG, UKG), only a small interaction is done. For higher classes, a basic test may be conducted.

Q: Can I pay fees in instalments?

A: Yes, fees can generally be paid in 3 instalments in one academic year.
